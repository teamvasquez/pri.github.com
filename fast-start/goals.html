WHAT ARE YOUR GOALS & DREAMS?

The first step on the path to success is to DREAM IT. Creating goals — both business and personal — are important. Take a moment to write down what you want to achieve, both in the near-term and long-term. It's also important to assign a date to your goals and share them with your Field Trainer, Accountability Partner, and Family/Friend.

Before you begin: The following 17 questions should take about 10–15 minutes. There are no right or wrong answers. If you're struggling with a question or running out of time, simply type “Need help/more time to complete” and keep moving. Your Field Trainer can help you come back to it later.

THE GAP

Where are you today — and where do you want to be?
Every journey starts by recognizing the gap between where you are now and where you want to be. Take a moment to honestly think about your current situation and what you'd like to change or improve.

Think about your career, income, time, lifestyle, family, finances, and opportunities.

What would you like to be different…have more or less of?

YOUR WHY

Your WHY is what gives your goals meaning. When things get uncomfortable, your WHY helps you remember what you're working toward.

YOUR VISION

Dream beyond where you are today.
Your vision is a picture of the future you want to create. Don't worry yet about how you're going to get there. Give yourself permission to think bigger.

Income • Career/Business • Time • Family • Home • Travel • Retirement • Giving • Personal growth • Etc.

YOUR GOALS

Dream it. Write it down. Give it a date.
Turn your vision into specific goals. Think about both personal and business goals, and include a target date whenever possible.
