STEP 1

First 72 Hours

Get connected, get organized, and get your first actions on the calendar.

Use this page as your checklist. Come back and check off each step as you go.
  • Download and log into the Primerica app within 24 hours.
  • Opt in to stay plugged in: Text WIN to 73513. Make sure the mobile number used to opt in is listed for your POL account: MyPOL → All Apps → MyInfo → Cell to verify, add, or change your number.
  • Connect with your Field Trainer (FT) and schedule onboarding / first training.
  • Block your current weekly training schedule on your calendar.
  • Register for your prelicensing course. Note your target completion date and share it with your FT.
  • Make your list! Share with your FT and together find your Easy 5.
    Jot down your top 10–50 people who know and trust you. Quickly scan for ideas. Take out a notepad and jot down anyone who comes to mind. Don’t judge, discriminate, or decide for them. Goal is 10, but write down as many people as come to mind. Need more? Scan your contacts on your phone or Facebook.
  • Easy 5: Identify your top 5 people who know you, trust you, and are likely to help you train.
  • Call or text all Easy 5 with your FT.
  • Book your own first field-training appointment with your FT.
  • Complete CHAMP and share it with your FT ASAP — preferably within your first 3 days.
  • WHY did you join us? Start thinking about your GOALS and what you’d like to achieve by when.
WHY WE MEET WITH YOUR MARKET
We meet to educate them, show them value, and let them experience firsthand what we do. We are not trying to sell your friends and family anything. When they see how we help families, they can confidently send future referrals your way.

Licensing

Use current Primerica/POL and California Life License Checklist instructions; ask your Field Trainer if you need help navigating each/any step.

  • Register for your prelicensing course.
  • Complete required prelicensing coursework.
  • Submit required application/certificate documents as instructed.
  • Review approval instructions and exam information.
  • Register for the Life/State exam.
  • Study using approved resources.
  • Pass the Life/State exam — CONGRATULATIONS!!! 🎉
  • Complete fingerprinting / Live Scan and remaining appointment requirements as instructed.
  • Confirm your license and appointment status with your FT/RLC.
  • Wait until you receive your Primerica Approval Letter before soliciting business.
